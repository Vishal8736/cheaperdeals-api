Refund and Return Policy

If something is wrong

If your item arrives damaged, is not what you ordered, or never arrives, contact us within 14 days of delivery (or within 14 days after the latest estimated delivery date if it has not arrived) with your order number and a photo if relevant.

What we do

We will send a replacement or refund the amount you paid for the item. Refunds go back by the same method where possible (Razorpay), otherwise by UPI, bank transfer or the crypto address you tell us.

Change of mind

Because items are shipped from overseas, we cannot always accept change-of-mind returns. Tell us anyway: we will look at each case fairly.

Time

We answer within 2 working days. Refunds are started within 5 working days of agreeing them.

Questions? Write to our support email.
