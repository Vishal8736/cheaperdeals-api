Playbook: dropshipping ki saari strategies

Yahan wo saari strategies hain jo system tumhare liye khud chalata hai. Koi button dabane ki zarurat nahi: upar Start dabate hi ye har ghante apne aap lagti hain. Har strategy margin floor (tumhara minimum profit) ka dhyan rakhti hai, aur jo product tumne haath se price diya hai (manual override) ya jo Strategy agent chala raha hai, use nahi chhoodti.

1. Tiered markup (cost ke hisaab se alag markup)

Format: 0-20:80, 20-100:50, 100-500:35, 500+:25. Matlab: supplier cost $0 se $20 ke product par 80% markup, $20-$100 par 50%, $100-$500 par 35%, $500 se upar 25%. Sasta maal bechne mein fixed percent kam padta hai, mehenge maal par bada percent customer ko door karta hai: isliye tier. Jo range match na ho wahan Settings wala global markup ({{ markup }}%) lagta hai. Ye purane products ka price ek dum nahi badalta, sirf jab unki cost badle.

2. Demand pricing (jo tez bik raha hai use mehenga)

Pichhle itne dino ki sales gini jati hain (1 se 90).
Itne din mein itne units bik gaye to demand "high" maana jata hai. Har itne aur units par price ek step aur badhta hai.
Chhota rakho (3-5%). Zyada badhane par sales ruk sakti hain.
Normal price se is se zyada kabhi nahi badhega. Demand kam hote hi price apne aap normal par wapas aa jata hai. Stock 0 ho to price nahi badhta.

3. Clearance (jo nahi bik raha use sasta)

Product itne din se listed ho aur ek bhi order na aaya ho to clearance lagti hai (7 se 365).
Price itna kam hota hai, par tumhare minimum margin se neeche kabhi nahi. Sale aate hi price normal par wapas.

4. Sale calendar (Diwali, Black Friday...)

Format: Naam|shuru-tarikh|aakhri-tarikh|discount%, tarikh YYYY-MM-DD. Jaise Black Friday|2026-11-27|2026-11-30|15. In dino sab products (manual price wale chhodkar) itna sasta ho jate hain aur sale khatam hote hi apne aap normal. Minimum margin se neeche nahi jate. Do sale ek saath ho to bada discount lagta hai.

5. Sasta supplier chuno

Backup supplier agar itne % se zyada sasta ho aur uske paas stock ho, to wo main supplier ban jata hai (purana main backup ban jata hai). Fayda: tumhara margin badhta hai. Product page par pehle backup supplier set karna padta hai.

6. Auto-retire (bekaar products band)

Itne din mein ek bhi order na aaye to product store se hata (inactive) ho jata hai taaki catalog saaf rahe. Delete nahi hota: Products page se kabhi bhi wapas ON kar sakte ho.
