Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you order we collect your name, email, phone number and delivery address. If you subscribe to deal alerts we collect your email. We do not store card numbers: payments are handled by the payment method you choose (for example UPI, a crypto wallet, BTCPay or Razorpay).

How we use it

To take and deliver your order, to tell you about it, to give support, and (only if you subscribed) to send deal alerts. We do not sell your data.

Who sees it

Your name, address and phone are shared with our supplier and the courier only so the parcel can reach you. Payment partners see what they need to process your payment.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as the law or accounting requires. Ask us to delete your subscription or personal data and we will, unless we must keep it by law.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, and you can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email.

Questions? Write to our support email.
