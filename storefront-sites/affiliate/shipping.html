Shipping Policy

Where we ship

Only to the countries offered at checkout.

How long it takes

Items are shipped from our supplier's warehouse. Estimated delivery is about 10-25 days after payment. Customs and courier delays can add time.

Cost

Shipping is shown at checkout before you pay. Some orders ship free above a set amount.

Tracking

Every order has a status page. Use the link you received at checkout or the Track order button and your order number with your email. Tracking numbers appear there once the parcel ships.

Customs and duties

Import taxes or duties charged by your country, if any, are your responsibility unless we say otherwise at checkout.
